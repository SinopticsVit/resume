## 7.4. Рекомендация

**Primary: Гуанчжоу (Tianhe or Pazhou)** — HQ representative office
- Причины: близость к Чэнхаю (2h), Nansha port, lowest Grade A rent, direct flights to Moscow, developing Russian business community.
- Addressable cluster: toys, clothing, electronics — 80%+ DM China sourcing.

**Secondary: Shenzhen Nanshan or Dongguan** — QC/sourcing field office (shared workspace)
- 1–2 sourcing/QC staff co-located with supplier base.
- Day trips to Chenghai (Shantou) for toy factory visits.

**Reserve: Shanghai** — если потребуется HQ-level presence для переговоров с top-tier brands (Goodbaby, Pop Mart HQ).

**Hybrid model:**
```
Гуанчжоу (RO HQ, 2–3 staff) ←→ Шэньчжэнь/Чэнхай (field, 2–3 staff)
         ↓
    Nansha Port / Rail → Moscow
```

## 8.3. Top-5 рисков с планом митигации

1. **Геополитика:** расчёты в CNY через Bank of China; избегать USD-correspondent chains; legal structure review quarterly.
2. **IP protection:** register all TM in CNIPA before first factory visit; engage local IP law firm (King & Wood Mallesons).
3. **Supplier quality:** mandatory pre-shipment QC (AQL 2.5); approved vendor list; quarterly audits.
4. **Currency:** 50% CNY prepayment hedge via forwards; maintain 3-month OPEX reserve in CNY.
5. **Regulatory compliance:** retainer with Dezan Shira / local law firm; subscribe to China Briefing regulatory updates.
